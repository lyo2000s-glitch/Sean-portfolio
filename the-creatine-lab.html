Brand Philosophy

Bold varsity identity built for peak performance

The Creatine Lab project involved developing a cohesive brand identity tailored for the health and fitness market. This included logo design, packaging, and brand visuals that create a consistent and memorable presence.

The brand and logo design was crafted to reflect the owner's vision of bold, varsity-style branding. The logo features a distinctive mountain symbol, representing summits and peak performance, reinforcing the brand's strong association with sports and athletic achievement.
